STEELPLANT AI COMMAND CENTER | Python, Scikit-learn, Streamlit

• Built an end-to-end industrial decision-support prototype combining plant-wise KPI analytics, energy/gas intensity benchmarking, forecasting, anomaly detection and scenario simulation.
• Developed a Random Forest energy forecasting pipeline using production, equipment load, ambient conditions and lag/rolling features with chronological hold-out validation.
• Implemented Isolation Forest anomaly screening and permutation-importance explainability to identify abnormal consumption patterns and predictive drivers.
• Added a what-if efficiency simulator and automated engineering action queue to translate analytics into potential savings scenarios and investigation priorities.

INTERVIEW:
