SAIL INTERVIEW — ADVANCED PROJECT CHEAT SHEET

Q1. What is novel about your project?
A: It is a decision-support loop, not only a dashboard: Monitor → Predict → Detect → Explain → Simulate → Recommend.

Q2. Why energy intensity instead of raw energy?
A: Production changes the absolute consumption. kWh/t normalizes energy against output and makes periods/units more comparable.

Q3. Why Isolation Forest?
A: It is unsupervised and useful when labelled examples of wastage/failure are unavailable. It flags unusual combinations of intensity and operating variables for engineering review.

Q4. Why Random Forest?
A: It is a strong tabular-data baseline and can capture nonlinear interactions without requiring heavy preprocessing.

Q5. Why chronological split?
A: Random shuffling can leak future patterns into training. A chronological split better represents real forecasting deployment.

Q6. What is permutation importance?
A: It measures how model performance changes when one feature is shuffled. It indicates predictive influence, not causal influence.

Q7. How would you deploy this at a steel plant?
A: Connect authorized historian/SCADA/ERP sources, establish data quality checks, retrain using plant-specific data, benchmark against current operating rules, validate alerts with domain engineers, and then deploy with monitoring.

Q8. How do you detect gas wastage?
A: First normalize gas consumption by production, then identify abnormal intensity after considering operating conditions. Flagged periods require engineering checks such as leakage, abnormal process operation, meter issues or equipment conditions.

Q9. How does the what-if simulator work?
A: It applies a user-selected percentage reduction to baseline energy consumption and converts the estimated reduction into indicative cost savings using an assumed energy price.

Q10. Biggest limitation?
A: Synthetic data cannot prove plant-level performance. Real deployment needs authorized data, process constraints, domain validation and monitored pilot testing.

Q11. What did you personally build?
A: Data generation/handling, feature engineering, KPI calculations, forecasting model, anomaly model, explainability layer, scenario simulator, recommendation logic and Streamlit interface.
